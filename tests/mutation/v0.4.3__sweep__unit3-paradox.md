The cap of two rounds is reached.

## Caveat

A kill proves the suite noticed *this* change, not that it tests the right thing. **A clean sweep is evidence about the mutations tried, not proof of suite completeness.** Tiger's real flags and exit codes are not exercised: the hook calls `<game>-tiger <mod-folder>` and treats any exit as advisory. Checking that against an installed `ck3-tiger` is a checklist item for a person.
